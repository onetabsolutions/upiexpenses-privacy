Privacy Policy

UPI Expenses Analyzer

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

1. Introduction

One Tab Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates UPI Expenses Analyzer ("the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it to analyze your UPI transaction statements and spending information.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and financial information.

2. Important Privacy Summary

UPI Expenses Analyzer is designed to process imported transaction data locally on your device.

The App does not require you to provide your UPI PIN, bank password, debit card PIN, or other banking credentials.

The App currently supports importing transaction information from supported CSV files for expense analysis.

3. Information You Import

You may choose to import a CSV file containing transaction information into the App.

Depending on the CSV file you provide, the imported information may include information such as:

  • Transaction date and time
  • Transaction amount
  • Transaction type
  • Transaction description
  • Merchant or recipient information
  • Transaction reference information
  • Categories or other transaction-related information

The information contained in an imported CSV file is determined by the source from which you obtained the file.

4. Local / Offline Processing

The App is designed to process imported transaction information locally on your device.

Your imported transaction data is used within the App to provide features such as transaction history, categorization, spending analysis, reports, and other expense-management functionality.

Your imported transaction information is not intentionally uploaded by the App to One Tab Solutions' servers for transaction analysis.

5. Data Storage

Transaction information imported into the App may be stored locally on your device so that the App can provide its features and retain your transaction history.

Local information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove it using functionality provided by the App or your device.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device.

6. Data Sharing

One Tab Solutions does not sell your imported transaction data.

We do not intentionally share the transaction information you import into the App with advertisers, banks, payment providers, or other third parties for their own purposes.

Because the App is designed for local processing, transaction analysis can be performed on the device without requiring the transaction data to be sent to our servers.

7. Banking and Payment Credentials

UPI Expenses Analyzer does not ask you to provide your:

  • UPI PIN
  • Banking password
  • ATM/debit card PIN
  • Internet banking credentials
  • Credit card PIN
  • Bank account login credentials

You should never enter sensitive banking credentials into the App or into an imported CSV file for the purpose of using the App.

8. App Permissions

The App may request access to device storage or file-selection functionality when you choose to import a CSV file.

Such access is used to allow you to select and import files that you explicitly choose to provide to the App.

The App does not require access to your banking application, UPI PIN, or bank account credentials.

9. Analytics and Diagnostics

If analytics, crash reporting, or other diagnostic services are enabled in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated to describe the relevant data practices before or when those features are introduced.

The current version of the App is designed primarily for local transaction analysis.

10. Advertising

If advertising is introduced into the App in a future version, the relevant advertising and data practices will be disclosed through the applicable Google Play Data Safety information and updates to this Privacy Policy.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to design the App so that imported transaction information can be processed locally on your device.

However, no electronic storage system or software application can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use appropriate device security, including a screen lock, password, PIN, biometric authentication, and other security features provided by your device.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

The App may rely on services provided by Google Play and the Android operating system for distribution, updates, security, and related platform functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

You control which CSV files you choose to import into the App.

You may remove imported transaction information from the App using the available deletion or data-management functionality, or by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App where applicable.

Before deleting App data or uninstalling the App, consider whether you need to retain your transaction history.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our data practices.
